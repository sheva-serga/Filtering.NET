# FN0014 — Interceptor declared without matching `[Map]`

**Severity:** Error

## What triggers it

A method has `[InterceptValue("Name")]` but no `[Map(nameof(User.Name))]` is declared on the same filter class.

## Why it matters

The interceptor only runs as part of the leaf-value extraction step for its target property. Without a corresponding `[Map]` there's nothing to attach the interceptor to.

## Fix

Add the matching `[Map]`, or remove the orphan interceptor.
